Studio.

TTSChatterbox Turbo● ⚙ Engines Operational · http://127.0.0.1:17494

Studio turns a project into produced audio in three steps — choose voices in Cast, let the LLM work out who speaks each line in Script, then batch-generate in Render. Works the same for audiobooks, game dialogue, and podcasts.

Smart-assign asks your LLM to match each role's name and description against the voice library and propose a cast. Override any pick manually — select a card, then click a voice in the library. Click an assigned voice again to unassign it.
