Appended to every line's own direction in this chapter, never instead of it. A scene may only add things that stack — which is why it takes words and effects but no numbers and no emotion: those would have to overwrite each persona's, and then nobody would sound like themselves any more.
Stacks on top of each persona's own chain, so a persona with reverb keeps it and gets the scene's as well.
Only Qwen3 CustomVoice reads the direction. For a persona on Kokoro, a clone, or Chatterbox Multilingual it does nothing — here that is the Narrator and Marius. The effects chain still applies to everyone, which is what makes a scene treatment worth having at all.
