How to read the marks

narration Prose. Never sent to the model, never in question — so it recedes. A paragraph's spoken part is dark and its narration grey, and the grey part is read by the Narrator.
tag / propagated The prose named the speaker, or it carried from a line that did. Anchors beat the model — its own pick is kept, and a disagreement is flagged.
llm 0.94 The model chose. Most spoken lines are decided this way. The number is the model's opinion of itself, and it has been 1.00 and wrong — so no score outranks a flag.
✓ you You set or confirmed this, and Analyze leaves it alone.
⚑ flagged Checks on the text, not on the model's confidence: three in a row, a persona with one line, a speaker not yet in the chapter, dialogue on the Narrator, an anchor the model disagrees with.
no speaker The model wasn't sure — under the floor — or skipped the line. Blocks rendering either way.

Direction, takes, rewriting a line in its persona's voice and rendering are not on this page. Script decides who says what; how it is performed is Render.
