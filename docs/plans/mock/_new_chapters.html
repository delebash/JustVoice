What these columns mean

Anchored The prose named the speaker — “said Marius” — or it carried from a line that did. Most speech has no anchor: “she said” and a gesture before the line name nobody.
Guessed The model chose. Most spoken lines are decided this way, so a guess alone is not a warning — the model can be sure and wrong, and its score says nothing about which.
Flagged Checks on the text, not on the model's confidence: three lines in a row by one persona, a persona with a single line, a speaker not yet in the chapter, dialogue on the Narrator, an anchor the model disagrees with. These are the lines worth your eyes.
No speaker The model wasn't sure, or skipped the line. These block rendering.
Renn added since Analyzed before that persona joined the cast, in a chapter that names them. Re-analyze it — lines you set are kept.
no dialogue found Nothing in the text was read as speech. Speech in ‘single quotes’, «guillemets» or after a dash isn't read as dialogue, so every line went to the Narrator.
can't re-cut The text changed after takes were recorded. Analyzing would re-cut the lines and delete those takes, so it stops.
failed The model call failed. The reason is on the row, and nothing was saved.
