How to read the marks

narration Prose. Never sent to the model, never in question — so it recedes.
tag / propagated The prose named the speaker, or it carried from a line that did. Anchors beat the model, so its own pick is kept but not used.
llm 0.94 A guess. The number is the model's opinion of itself, and it has been 1.00 and wrong — so the mark is the same at every score.
⚑ flagged Checks on the text, not on the model: three in a row, a persona with one line, a speaker not yet in the scene, dialogue on the Narrator, an anchor that went stale.
floored Under the confidence floor, or no answer at all. Blocks rendering either way.

Direction, takes and rendering are not on this page. Script decides who says what; how it is performed is Render.

Appended to every line's own direction in this chapter, never instead of it. A scene may only add things that stack — which is why it takes words and effects but no numbers and no emotion: those would have to overwrite each persona's, and then nobody would sound like themselves any more.

Speech enginesVRAM 3.2 / 8.6 GB Kokoro
One speech engine is resident at a time. Loading another unloads the current one — that's why auditioning across engines costs a swap, and why every listen button in the app warns before it charges you a minute.

Picking an engine is picking what a voice can do

You choose an engine once, when you cast a persona to a voice. From then on it decides three things for every line that persona speaks: whether you can clone their timbre, whether they can be directed in words, and whether a per-line emotion reaches the model. Nothing else in the app can put back what the engine doesn't do.

If you want…UseWhat you give up
Ready-made voices, instantly, on any machine Kokoro — 54 presets No cloning, and no direction of any kind. Pace, pitch, gain and pauses only.
To direct performances in prose Qwen3 CustomVoice — 9 presets Cannot clone. You are limited to its nine timbres.
Someone's own voice, and per-line emotion Chatterbox Turbo — cloning Written direction is ignored. English only.
Someone's own voice, in another language Chatterbox Multilingual — 23 languages No direction and no emotion — timbre and the numbers.
Someone's own voice, on a machine with no GPU LuxTTS — cloning, English No direction and no emotion.
