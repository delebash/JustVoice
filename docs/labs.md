# Labs

Five measurement and tuning surfaces under one tab. They share a rule: evidence
over vibes — every verdict comes from a real render you can listen to.

- **Compare** — A/B two renders of the same text (different voices, engines, or
  settings) with analyzer verdicts beside your ears.
- **Train** — the LoRA training queue: fine-tune a voice from samples; finished
  adapters land in your voice library like any other voice.
- **Speaker Lab** — tune speaker attribution: race two configurations
  (prompt / tier / confidence floor) over the same text, two columns, pick the
  winner.
- **Render Lab** — a settings matrix (up to 16 cells, two rendering
  concurrently) for hunting the right delivery parameters.
- **Audio Tools** — one-off audio utilities (analyze a file, master a file)
  without touching a project.

Old bookmarks keep working: the retired `/compare`, `/train`, `/speakerlab`,
`/renderlab`, and `/audio` addresses redirect here, and `/cache`, `/channels`,
`/webhooks` redirect into Settings.
