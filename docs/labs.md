# Labs

Four measurement and tuning surfaces under one tab — the voice domain. They
share a rule: evidence over vibes — every verdict comes from a real render you
can listen to.

- **Compare** — A/B two renders of the same text (different voices, engines, or
  settings) with analyzer verdicts beside your ears.
- **Train** — the LoRA training queue: fine-tune a voice from samples; finished
  adapters land in your voice library like any other voice.
- **Render Lab** — a settings matrix (up to 16 cells, two rendering
  concurrently) for hunting the right delivery parameters.
- **Audio Tools** — one-off audio utilities (analyze a file, master a file)
  without touching a project.

Tuning **speaker attribution** lives on the AI Settings page now: Routing by
feature → the attribution row's **Lab**, which runs the same reading pipeline
as Studio's Analyze — race configurations in columns, correct results in
place, and every correction teaches the next run
(see [AI features](ai-features.md)).

Old bookmarks keep working: the retired `/compare`, `/train`, `/renderlab`,
and `/audio` addresses redirect here, `/speakerlab` lands on the AI Settings
Lab, and `/cache`, `/channels`, `/webhooks` redirect into Settings.
